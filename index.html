Community standards

Connection starts with respect.

Marigat Connect is an adults-only social community. Members are expected to respect one another, protect personal information and engage responsibly.

Members will have access to reporting and blocking tools. Harassment, exploitation and unlawful activities are not permitted. Marigat Connect does not broker paid sexual services or charge for sexual encounters.
